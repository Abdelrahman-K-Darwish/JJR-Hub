The Cohort Program
The entry point into JEDI CAB
Cohort Model

Small groups of 4–6 consultants selected annually. Each cohort runs 6 months with structured learning, mentorship, and a capstone project that solves a real firm challenge. Not everyone joins — and that's intentional.

Learn More →
Mentorship & Pairing

Each participant is paired with a senior leader for bi-weekly 1:1 sessions structured around career goals, equity practice, and real project challenges. Tracked in the Hub for continuity.

Meet Mentors →
Capstone Projects

Real problems, real impact. Past capstones produced the EJ assessment toolkit, revised onboarding flow, and the consultant satisfaction survey that leadership now tracks quarterly.

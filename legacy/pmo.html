Client discovery phase needs more structure
What Worked
Adding a structured discovery questionnaire to the Acme engagement cut the scoping phase by 2 weeks. Recommend standardising this for all strategy projects.
S. Foster · Acme Corp 18 Mar 2026 Strategy · Active Projects
Scope creep signals missed in early sprints
Challenge
NovaTech engagement had informal scope additions in sprint 2 that weren't caught until sprint 4. Need a scope change trigger in sprint reviews.
F. Nakamura · NovaTech 10 Mar 2026 Operations · PM Process
EJ screening data should be centralised
Recommendation
The GovServices team recreated EJ screening datasets that Equity & Impact CoP already had. Recommend linking CoP resource libraries to project workspaces.
